Priority Definitions

BUILD (Critical)

Strong market signal validation. Customers are asking for it. Closes competitive gap. Ship within 8-12 weeks.

INVESTIGATE

Shows promise but needs deeper validation. Run user research or small pilot. Decision point in 6-8 weeks.

DEFER

Lower priority. Limited market signal. Not blocking any deals. Revisit after core BUILD items ship.
