Why 2025 became an inflection point

Marketing operations reached an inflection point in 2025. Enterprise content production stayed stubbornly manual (4 hours per page in AEM despite $500K+ CMS investments) while competitors launched agentic AI assistants and platforms. Simultaneously, compliance requirements intensified: EU Accessibility Act in force, WCAG 2.1 AA as global legal standard, and enterprises demanding 99.9% compliance accuracy across every published page.

Enterprises faced a choice: hire more QA/compliance staff to keep pace with content scale, or find a fundamentally different way to orchestrate content production.
