Messaging Principles

⚡
Lead with Business Impact
Start with measurable outcomes (80% time reduction, 20x efficiency, 3-5x scale) before explaining the agentic architecture.
🔒
Emphasize Compliance as Existential
5,000+ ADA lawsuits in 2025, EU Accessibility Act in force, WCAG 2.1 AA. Automated compliance is not optional—it's insurance.
🤖
Agentic Execution vs. Copilot Assistance
Competitors are copilots (humans direct step-by-step). Gradial deploys autonomous agents (24/7 execution, zero training required).
🏢
Vendor Independence Matters
Adobe bundles (CX Enterprise lock-in). Sitecore is monolithic. Gradial stays on top of your AEM, Salesforce, Sitecore stack.
💰
ROI via Headcount Avoidance
Stop hiring QA specialists and compliance auditors. Redirect budget to Gradial. Scale 5x with same team. CFOs love this math.
📊
Talk to Fortune 500 Scale
Not a startup platform. 9 Fortune 500 companies in production (T-Mobile, Cisco, Intel, Wells Fargo, Kaiser). Proven at scale.
